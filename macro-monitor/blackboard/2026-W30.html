  • Divergence: CPI +4.2% y/y / +0.5% m/m headline vs core +0.2% m/m (under +0.3% est); PPI +1.1% m/m / +6.5% y/y (hottest since Nov '22) vs core +0.4% m/m (under +0.5% est). Consumer core-commodity prices fell −0.1%.

Forward risk is conditional on oil (Iran) staying elevated.
  • (2) Supply-side, not demand-driven: a commodity/tariff shock; the demand-slowing tool can't fix an oil price.
  • (3) Fed: 2026 cuts off (≈72/102 economists see a hold at 3.50–3.75%; ~96–97% hold odds at Jun 16–17 — Warsh's first meeting as chair, sworn in May 22). A hike is a ~6% tail, not a base case — watch Warsh's tone/guidance, not whether the dot plot exists.
