1 · Eurozone HICP-flash date over-precise — corrected (calendar)

The draft week-ahead placed the September Eurozone HICP flash on Wed 30 Sep. Eurostat releases the flash estimate on the last working day of the reference month or the first working day of the next, so a hard Sep-30 date was falsely precise. Resolution: moved to the ~1 Oct window (grouped with the Thu 1 Oct US claims / ISM Manufacturing block) in both the published report and the blackboard synthesis before publishing.

2 · September NFP consensus rounding — corrected (figure)

The draft stated the September payrolls consensus as ~+53K, a round of the blackboard's logged FactSet figure of ~+52.5K. Resolution: tightened to the exact ~+52.5K in the report and synthesis so the figure ties precisely to the Labor section's logged value.

3 · August JOLTS release date — flagged, deferred (calendar)

The Labor domain logs August JOLTS on Tue 29 Sep; the generic release cadence (JOLTS in the first week of a month, ~2-month data lag) would instead place the August report in early October. The two sources disagree. Because Part D bars a web lookup for the week-ahead and the blackboard is the authoritative source — with the Labor desk owning the JOLTS schedule and Sep 29 being a valid Tuesday — the date was kept as logged and flagged for transparency rather than overridden. This residual, unconfirmed date-provenance tension is the −1 on Event Date & Calendar Accuracy.
