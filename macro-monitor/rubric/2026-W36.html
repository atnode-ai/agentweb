Issue 1 — Attribution: Warsh's "first" FOMC

A draft of the Week Ahead billed the 16 Sep FOMC as "Warsh's first rate decision chairing." That is wrong: Kevin Warsh was sworn in on 22 May 2026, the 16-17 June FOMC was his first meeting as chair, and 29 July was his second. September is his third meeting — his first with a genuine hike on the table, but not his first rate decision. Corrected to "Warsh chairing, a live hike on the table."
