Issue — Jackson Hole keynote date

The briefing dated Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole keynote to 29 August. That date is a Saturday in 2026; the Kansas City Fed symposium's keynote is delivered on the Friday, 28 Aug 2026. The error appeared three times — twice as “Fri 29 Aug” in the body and once as a starred “Sat 29” item in the Week Ahead — and contradicted the report's own “week ending Friday, 28 August” framing.

Correction applied

All three instances were changed to Fri 28 Aug and the corrected edition republished. The briefing is now internally consistent on the keynote date.

Verified clean

Other dated references check out: WEEK ENDING Friday, 28 August ✓, 4 Sep August payrolls (first-Friday NFP) ✓, ECB 10 Sep (Thursday) ✓, July PCE 26 Aug ✓, Aug PCE 30 Sep ✓.
